## Overview

Customer Hierarchy structures customer records into a parent-child model — a top-level **Group**, with **Sub-groups** or **Merchants** nested beneath it. Pricing rules, caps, and commitments set on a parent cascade down to its children automatically, and billing can consolidate at the parent level regardless of which child recorded the underlying activity.

## Setting Up Hierarchy

Hierarchy is configured as part of the customer creation wizard, in the **Hierarchy Structure** step.

### 1. Navigate to Add Customer

`Billing > Customers > Add Customer`

<Frame>
  {/* Screenshot: Billing > Customers dashboard with the Add Customer button */}

  ![Navigate to Add Customer](/images/add-customer-navigation.png)
</Frame>

### 2. Complete Basic and Contact Information

Advance through **Basic Information** and **Contact Information** to reach **Hierarchy Structure** on the left-side stepper.

### 3. Configure Hierarchy Structure

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Hierarchy Level**_(Required)_ | Structural tier of this record — e.g. `Group`, `Sub-group`, `Merchant` |
| **Parent Customer**_(Conditional)_ | The parent entity this record rolls up to. Required for any non-root node; search selects from existing customer records |

<Frame>
  {/* Screenshot: Hierarchy Structure step with Hierarchy Level and Parent Customer fields */}

  ![Hierarchy Structure fields](/images/hierarchy-structure-config.png)
</Frame>

### 4. Save

Continue through **Billing**, **Tax Configuration**, and **Custom Attributes**, then **Save** to create the record.

## Key Workflow Considerations

### Root Nodes

The top-most record in a hierarchy (e.g. a corporate Group) has no Parent Customer.

### Billing Levels

Billing can be enabled at this specific level or left to roll up to an ancestor. This determines where invoices consolidate — not necessarily at the level where the transaction activity was recorded.

### Cascading Rules

Pricing rules, caps, and commitments configured on a parent apply to every child beneath it without re-configuration at each level.
