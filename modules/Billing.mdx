## What Billing Does

Billing handles pricing configuration, charge creation, approval workflows, invoice generation, and credit notes. It is one of ZenStatement's two product lines, alongside Accounts Receivable.

It is structured as a fixed chain of stages, each requiring approval or validation before the next stage can begin:

**Customers → Plans → Charges → Transactions → Billing Calculations → Invoicing & Credit Notes**

## Goals This Module Fulfills

### Governance

Two-role (maker-checker) approval at customer creation, charge creation, billing calculation, and invoice/credit note dispatch.

### Customer Hierarchy

Cascade pricing rules, caps, and commitments from a parent customer to child customers without re-configuration at each level.

### Multi-Currency

Lock an FX rate per customer for consistent billing across cycles.

### Price Escalations

Apply scheduled price increases (Flat %, Flat Amount, Tier-based, or Target Price) without manual re-entry each cycle.

### Pricing Models

Support fixed value, fixed percentage, flat/volume tiered, progressive tiered, and retrospective (post-period) pricing.

### Formula-Based Charges

Calculate charges using custom formulas built on transaction file attributes.

### Transaction Filtering

Filter which transaction rows a given charge applies to, so one uploaded file can feed multiple charges.

### File Validation

Validate uploaded transaction files through three gates — intake, schema, integrity — before use in billing.

### Flexible Cadence

Set a billing cadence per charge, independent of the plan-level cadence.

### Lifecycle Tracking

Track charge status through a defined lifecycle: Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected, Partially Rejected, Pending Invoicing, Invoiced.

### Calculation Approval

Require a separate approval on billing calculations before invoice generation, in addition to charge-level approvals.

### Audit Trail

Maintain creator, approver, and dispatch timestamp on every invoice and credit note.
